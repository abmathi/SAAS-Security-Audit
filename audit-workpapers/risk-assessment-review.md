# Risk Assessment Review

**Framework Alignment**
- SOC 2
- NIST CSF 2.0

## Control Objective
Identify, assess, and manage cybersecurity risks affecting the SaaS environment.

## Risk Assessment Scope
- Identity and access management
- Logging and monitoring
- Vendor management
- Cloud security
- Incident response readiness

## Evidence Collected
- Risk register
- Risk ratings
- Mitigation tracking documentation

## Review Activities Performed
- Identified operational and technical risks
- Evaluated likelihood and business impact
- Assigned risk owners
- Reviewed mitigation status

## Findings
Several medium and high risks were identified requiring continued remediation tracking.

## Recommendations
- Enforce MFA organization-wide
- Improve centralized logging visibility
- Conduct vendor security reviews
- Perform incident response tabletop exercises
- Establish periodic access reviews
