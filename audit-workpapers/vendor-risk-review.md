# Vendor Risk Management Review

**Framework Alignment**
- SOC 2
- NIST CSF 2.0

## Control Objective
Identify and manage cybersecurity risks associated with third-party vendors and SaaS providers.

## Vendors Reviewed
- GitHub
- AWS
- Google Workspace
- Cloudflare
- Slack
- OpenAI

## Evidence Collected
- Vendor inventory
- Risk classifications
- Criticality assignments
- Security review tracking

## Review Activities Performed
- Identified critical third-party providers
- Evaluated vendor risk exposure
- Reviewed vendor dependency impact
- Assessed security review status

## Findings
Several critical third-party providers were identified that support core SaaS business operations.

Some vendors had not yet completed formalized security review processes.

## Recommendations
- Establish recurring vendor security assessments
- Track SOC 2 reports from critical vendors
- Review vendor access permissions periodically
- Formalize third-party risk management procedures
