# Asset Inventory Review

**Framework Alignment**
- SOC 2
- NIST CSF 2.0

## Control Objective
Maintain an accurate inventory of systems, applications, and SaaS services to support security governance and risk management.

## Assets Reviewed
- SaaS platforms
- Cloud infrastructure
- Identity providers
- Communication systems
- Security services

## Evidence Collected
- Asset inventory spreadsheet
- Service ownership assignments
- Data sensitivity classifications
