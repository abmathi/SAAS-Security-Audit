# Incident Response Policy

## Purpose
Establish procedures for identifying, responding to, and recovering from security incidents.

## Policy
- Security incidents must be reported immediately.
- Incident severity must be classified.
- Evidence must be preserved during investigations.
- Lessons learned reviews should occur after incidents.

## Response Phases
1. Identification
2. Containment
3. Eradication
4. Recovery
5. Lessons Learned
