# Access Control Policy

## Purpose
Define requirements for logical access management within the SaaS environment.

## Policy
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) must be enabled for all privileged accounts.
- Access must follow the principle of least privilege.
- Shared accounts are prohibited.
- Administrative access must be reviewed periodically.
- User access must be removed promptly upon termination.

## Enforcement
Violations may result in access removal or disciplinary action.
